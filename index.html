From photograph to memory

Three simple steps.

01

Choose your photograph

Capture a printed photograph or choose a reference image. Crop and straighten it to prepare your memory.

02

Attach the moment

Record or import a video, trim your favorite part, and save it with the photograph.

03

Go down Memory Lane

Point your camera at the same printed photograph on a supported device. Your video plays over it in AR.

A glimpse of Memory Lane

The moment
comes back.

Move toward the framed photograph and see its attached video begin to play.

This illustrative recording uses a simulated camera feed on the app's AR page. It explains the idea; real recognition uses your device's camera and a physical photograph.

AR needs compatible hardware, good lighting and a suitable reference photograph. Saved videos can also be watched fullscreen.

Made for your memories

A library that feels like yours.

More moments per photo

Attach several clips to one photograph. Choose which one plays in AR or randomize playback.

Your own people

Keep separate libraries for up to three people, with names and avatars.

Make it fit

Adjust overlay position, size and rotation. Choose video quality and control playback sound.

Local by design

Your library stays on your device.

No Memories account is needed. The app stores imported media and settings locally; it does not upload your library to a developer server.
