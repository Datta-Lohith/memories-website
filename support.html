Getting started

  1. Complete the introduction and enter a profile name with at least three letters.
  2. Tap + in Library. Capture or choose a photograph, crop if needed, then attach a video and save.
  3. Open Memory Lane using the Library camera button. Point at the same printed photograph on a supported iPhone or iPad.
  4. For several clips on one photograph, open its Library menu → Videos & AR playback. Choose a clip or random playback.

After name entry, the interactive tutorial introduces the main controls. Replay it from Menu → Help → Interactive tutorial.

Common questions

How do I record and share Memory Lane?

Open Memory Lane with a saved memory and point at the reference photograph. Tap the rings button at the bottom to begin recording; the red Stop button and timer show that recording is active. Tap Stop, preview the finished clip, then choose Share or Save to Photos. Photos saving asks for add-only permission. The recording uses the memory video's sound when available and unmuted; it does not record your microphone.

How do I add another video to the same photograph?

Tap + in Library and choose Add to existing memory, then select the saved photograph. Use Videos & AR playback in the memory menu to manage its videos and choose selected or randomized playback. Similar-photo checks can suggest an existing entry, but difficult crops or camera angles may still need your confirmation.

Can I combine entries or download the saved photograph?

Use Library's edit pencil, select entries for the same photograph, and choose Merge to bring their videos together. Review your selection before confirming. To save an exact copy of a memory's reference photograph, open its menu and choose Download Memory image, then choose where to save it in Files.

What happens during a call or screen sharing?

Video capture opens the camera before requesting microphone access. During calls or screen sharing, new memory videos record without microphone sound. If another app owns the camera, use the gallery option or end that camera activity and retry. Memory Lane recordings use the attached video's sound rather than the microphone.

How do app update reminders work?

The public App Store app can check for a newer compatible version once daily. A reminder appears when Library is idle: Update opens the App Store, while Later or dismissing postpones reminders for three days. The library remains usable offline. TestFlight builds use TestFlight's own notifications and automatic-update options.

The Memory Lane camera button is disabled

Save at least one memory first. Memory Lane needs a reference photograph and attached video to recognize and play.

The camera is unavailable or slow

Check camera permission in Settings for Memories. Finish any active capture and return to Library before reopening Memory Lane. If the camera is interrupted, return to Library and try again. Stop screen sharing or recording to check whether another capture session is involved; include those conditions in a report if the problem persists. Restart the app or device if necessary.

The photograph is not recognized

Use the same physical photograph as your saved reference. Try an evenly lit, detailed print with little glare, hold the device steady, and keep the whole photo in view. Crop the reference to the photograph's edges. AR needs compatible hardware; simulator demonstrations do not perform real camera tracking.

The video is rotated, too large or in the wrong position

Open the memory's Library menu and its advanced overlay controls. Adjust rotation, position and size while reviewing the overlay example, then save. Default playback fits the video's proportions; your saved adjustments can allow it to extend beyond the photograph.

I cannot hear the video

Check device volume and the Menu's memory mute setting. Fullscreen video playback also provides its own mute/unmute control for that viewing session.

Video import or trimming fails

Make sure a cloud-hosted Photos/Files item has downloaded and can play in its original app. Try another playable MP4, MOV or M4V file and check free storage. In the trim editor, select the start/end range and attach it; canceling keeps the previous attachment.

Auto-crop does not find the edges

Automatic detection works best with a clear photograph boundary and contrast against its background. Move the corner handles manually when detection is unsuitable, and leave a sufficiently detailed image rather than a very small crop.

How do I change a person's name or remove a profile?

Open Menu and use the profile's pencil button or hold the profile to open its edit options. Profile names must be different. Deleting a profile removes its library; keep original media elsewhere before deleting.

Where are my memories stored? Are they backed up?

Imported media is stored locally in the app. The app has no developer cloud-sync service. Apple device backups may include it depending on your settings; offloading and deleting the app behave differently. Keep original copies of important media. See the privacy policy.

I am running out of storage

Check available storage and the video-quality preference under Menu → App settings. Balanced is the default; Smaller files reduces newly prepared videos. Trim unnecessary footage or delete unwanted memories. Changing quality does not recompress existing saved videos.

What to include in a report

Tell us the app version/build, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, what you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it. Mention whether you were screen sharing/recording and which permissions were enabled. Include only a non-sensitive error message or anonymized sample if necessary.

Protect your private media

Support cannot retrieve your local library from a developer server. Do not post personal photos, videos, profile names, account information, keys or passwords in the public tracker.
