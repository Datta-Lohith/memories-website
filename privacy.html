Privacy policy for Memories: Re-Live for iPhone and iPad.

Effective date: October 11, 2026 · Developer: Datta Lohith

At a glance

The app does not require a developer account, use advertising or developer analytics, or upload your library to a developer-operated service. Your photos, videos, profile names and preferences are processed and stored locally.

What the app stores and why

Memories: Re-Live stores the photographs and videos you select or capture, optional memory titles, local profile names and avatars, and settings such as appearance, sound, import source and video quality. It also stores local metadata needed to organize memories, select videos, place overlays, manage media and schedule review requests.

This information supports your library, editing, playback and separate person profiles on the device. Profile names identify local libraries; they do not create online accounts. AR recognition and media preparation run on the device. Temporary files may be created during capture, import, trimming and playback.

Camera, microphone, Photos and Files

  • Camera: used to capture reference photographs or videos and recognize saved photographs for AR playback. Live AR camera frames are processed on the device and are not sent to the developer.
  • Microphone: used to record sound when you record a memory video.
  • Photos and Files: system pickers provide the media you choose. The app imports copies for its local library; it does not need unrestricted access to your entire photo library.

You can change camera and microphone permissions in your device's Settings. Denying a permission may prevent the corresponding capture feature; importing media and saved-video playback remain available where supported.

Storage, retention and deletion

Saved memories remain on the device until you delete them or remove the app. Deleting a memory removes that memory's photo/video associations; shared media can remain while another memory or profile uses it. The app also cleans unused temporary media. Removing imported copies from Memories does not delete your original items from Photos or Files.

Deleting a profile removes its local library; media shared with another profile can remain. Use the Library and profile deletion controls to remove content. Deleting the app removes its local app container; offloading the app may preserve data. Keep your own copies of important media.

Device backups and external providers

Depending on your device settings, app data may be included in Apple's device or iCloud backups. Memories does not provide its own cloud sync or backup service. A Photos or Files provider may download selected media from its cloud service before import. Those providers and Apple handle that activity under their own policies; the developer does not receive your library through these services.

Apple services and diagnostics

App Store downloads, ratings/reviews, TestFlight feedback and operating-system diagnostics are handled by Apple. Memories may request a system review prompt; the app does not receive your review text or know whether you submitted a review. Apple may provide developers with crash or diagnostic information according to your settings and its policies. The app does not add third-party advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs.

This website and support

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. It does not add analytics, advertising cookies, embedded third-party video players or a contact form. GitHub may process normal hosting information such as your IP address and request details under its privacy statement.

If you choose to contact support through GitHub Issues, your GitHub username, issue text and attachments are public. That information is used to investigate and respond to your request and may remain in the public issue history. Do not post private photographs, videos, names, credentials or sensitive diagnostics. You can edit your post and use GitHub's available account/content controls. GitHub's own policies apply to the service.

Children and personal media

The app does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a developer service. People may store personal or family media locally. A parent or guardian should supervise any public support submission by a child; do not include a child's identifying information or private media in an issue.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change, with the new effective date shown above. Questions about privacy can be raised through a privacy question on GitHub Issues. This is a public channel; describe the question without disclosing personal information.
